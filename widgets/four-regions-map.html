Critique the Map: Four Texases, Not One Desert
A published map that colors all of Texas the same shade of tan is hiding something real. Reveal the four regions to see what it is hiding.
"Texas: One Big Desert"
This is what a flattened, low-detail map shows. It is also wrong.
Piney Woods (East Texas)
💧 ~45+ inches of rain a year
Forested, well-watered. Home to the Caddo: settled, agricultural, and confederated.
Blackland Prairie (Central Texas)
💧 ~30–35 inches of rain a year
Fertile grassland, transitional between the wetter east and the drier west.
Hill Country / Edwards Plateau
💧 ~20–30 inches of rain a year
Rugged escarpment country. A real dividing line, not just a line on a map.
Llano Estacado (West Texas)
💧 ~15–20 inches of rain a year
Flat, dry, few landmarks. Home to the Querecho, Apache bands living here before the horse.
Rainfall figures are approximate long-term averages, used here to show the gradient, not exact historical measurements.
